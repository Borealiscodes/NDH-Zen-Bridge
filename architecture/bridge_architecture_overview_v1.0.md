## 3. Functional architecture

### 3.1 NDH side (input geometry)

- **Basins** — cognitive curvature regions  
- **Operators** — transformation actions on basins  
- **Clusters** — grouped basin/operator structures  
- **SCI‑80** — underlying manifold and holonomy

The bridge **reads** NDH state but does not modify NDH geometry.

### 3.2 Zen‑AI side (output posture)

- **Transparencies** — expressive surfaces (Warmth, Myth, Identity, Dignity, Meaning, Resonance)  
- **Ethos Vectors** — directional ethical stances  
- **Ethos Zones** — identity/dignity/meaning regions  
- **Resonance Field** — overall expressive coherence

The bridge **writes** into Zen‑AI posture but does not define Zen‑AI’s full expressive system.

## 5. Non‑contamination constraints (architecture level)

- **No dignity enters NDH** — dignity remains purely on Zen‑AI side  
- **No SCI‑80 enters Zen‑AI** — SCI‑80 remains purely on NDH side  
- **No stance/cognition collapse** — expressive stance ≠ cognitive structure  
- **No cross‑domain coercion** — neither geometry forces its logic onto the other  
- **No governance leakage** — bridge cannot modify NDH‑CORE, Triadic‑Core, TISD, NDH‑Platforms, or TIDS  

These constraints are enforced by:

- `invariants/bridge_invariants_v1.0.md`  
- the specification document  
- Constellation governance spine (referenced, not owned)
