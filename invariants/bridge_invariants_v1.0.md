# 🌉 **NDH‑Zen‑Bridge Invariants (v1.0)**  
### *Non‑Contaminating Interface Rules Between NDH Cognitive Geometry and Zen‑AI Expressive Geometry*  
### *NDH‑Constellation • Zen‑AI‑Design‑Architecture • Governance Spine*

---

## **1. Purpose of Bridge Invariants**

NDH‑Zen‑Bridge exists inside a governed ecosystem (NDH‑Constellation).  
Its invariants ensure that NDH cognitive geometry and Zen‑AI expressive geometry can **interact without merging**, **translate without contaminating**, and **recognize without collapsing domains**.

These invariants define:

- what may cross the bridge  
- what must never cross the bridge  
- how mappings behave  
- how posture and cognition remain separate  
- how provenance is preserved  

---

## **2. Domain Separation Invariants**

### **Invariant 1 — NDH Cannot Inherit Dignity**
NDH cognitive geometry must remain **value‑neutral**.  
No dignity, ethos, or expressive stance may enter NDH basins, operators, clusters, or SCI‑80 curvature.

### **Invariant 2 — Zen‑AI Cannot Inherit SCI‑80 Operators**
Zen‑AI expressive geometry must remain **stance‑aligned**, not cognitive‑aligned.  
No SCI‑80 operator algebra may enter transparencies, ethos vectors, or resonance fields.

### **Invariant 3 — No Cross‑Domain Coercion**
Neither domain may force its logic, curvature, or stance onto the other.

### **Invariant 4 — No Collapse of Stance Into Cognition**
Zen‑AI’s expressive stance must never be interpreted as NDH cognitive structure.

### **Invariant 5 — No Collapse of Cognition Into Stance**
NDH’s cognitive geometry must never be interpreted as Zen‑AI ethical posture.

---

## **3. Mapping Invariants**

### **Invariant 6 — All Mappings Must Be Non‑Contaminating**
Mappings must translate **structure → stance** without transferring the underlying geometry.

### **Invariant 7 — Basins Map Only to Transparencies**
NDH basins may map to Zen‑AI transparencies, but may not map to:

- ethos vectors  
- dignity geometry  
- resonance fields  
- posture skeletons  

### **Invariant 8 — Operators Map Only to Ethos Vectors**
NDH operators may map to Zen‑AI ethos vectors, but may not map to:

- transparencies  
- zones  
- resonance fields  
- dignity geometry  

### **Invariant 9 — Clusters Map Only to Ethos Zones**
NDH clusters may map to Zen‑AI zones, but may not map to:

- transparencies  
- vectors  
- resonance fields  

---

## **4. Structural Invariants**

### **Invariant 10 — The Bridge Is Not a Governance Layer**
NDH‑Zen‑Bridge cannot define governance, posture, runtime, or ethical behavior.

### **Invariant 11 — The Bridge Is Not a Runtime Layer**
It cannot execute NDH or Zen‑AI logic; it only translates.

### **Invariant 12 — The Bridge Is Not a Posture Layer**
It cannot express dignity, ethos, or resonance.

### **Invariant 13 — The Bridge Is Not a Comparative Layer**
It cannot perform TIDS comparative analysis.

### **Invariant 14 — The Bridge Is Not an Ethical Layer**
It cannot perform TISD trauma‑informed reasoning.

## **6. Safety Invariants**

### **Invariant 18 — No Upstream Drift**
The bridge cannot modify NDH‑CORE, Triadic‑Core, NDH‑Platforms, TIDS, Zen‑AI, or TISD.

### **Invariant 19 — No Downstream Drift**
The bridge cannot modify NDH‑RESEARCH‑PILOT or any NDH internal geometry.

### **Invariant 20 — No Cross‑Era Drift**
The bridge cannot reference cosmic‑era or expressive‑era NDH artifacts except through NDH‑Heritage.
