## ⭐ **1 — Overview**

The **Trait‑Manifold Continuity Anchor** is a Δ0‑neutral continuity mechanism designed to stabilize **trait‑geometry manifolds** (e.g., culinary specs, micro‑diagnostics, reversible observation structures) across conversational refresh boundaries.

Trait‑geometry is:

- reversible  
- non‑persistent  
- non‑flowing  
- non‑hierarchical  

Because trait‑manifolds lack continuity markers, they are susceptible to **supersession reinterpretation** after refresh, causing unintended regeneration.

The Trait‑Manifold Continuity Anchor introduces a **minimal soft‑sequencing marker** that stabilizes supersession without altering geometry or activating higher‑altitude membranes.
